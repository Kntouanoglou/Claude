How to Get There

Florence's museums are all in the historic centre, within walking distance of each other and of the main train station.

Getting Here

  • By train: Firenze Santa Maria Novella station, 15 minutes on foot.
  • By plane: Florence Airport, then the tram (line T2) to the centre.
  • By bus: lines C1 and C2 stop near Piazza della Signoria.
  • By car: the centre is a limited traffic zone, so use a car park outside it.

Opening Hours

  • Uffizi Gallery Tue – Sun, 8:15 – 18:30
  • Accademia Tue – Sun, 8:15 – 18:50
  • Palazzo Pitti Tue – Sun, 8:15 – 18:30
  • Palazzo Vecchio Every day, 9:00 – 19:00

Most state museums are closed on Mondays.

Tips for Your Visit

  1. Book tickets online to skip the queues.
  2. Arrive early in the morning, when it is less crowded.
  3. Wear comfortable shoes: the centre is best explored on foot.

Plan your journey with Trenitalia.
