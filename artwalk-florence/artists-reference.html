Masters of the Renaissance

The greatest artists of the Renaissance lived and worked in Florence. Meet some of them, and find out where to see their work in the city today.

Sandro Botticelli

1445 – 1510

Painter of graceful figures and mythological scenes, and a favourite of the Medici family.

  • The Birth of Venus – Uffizi Gallery
  • Primavera – Uffizi Gallery

Michelangelo Buonarroti

1475 – 1564

Sculptor, painter and architect. He carved David from a single block of marble at 26.

  • David – Galleria dell'Accademia
  • Doni Tondo – Uffizi Gallery

Leonardo da Vinci

1452 – 1519

Painter, inventor and scientist, trained in Florence in the workshop of Verrocchio.

  • Annunciation – Uffizi Gallery
  • Adoration of the Magi – Uffizi Gallery

A Walk Through the Renaissance

  1. Giotto designs the cathedral's bell tower (1334).
  2. Brunelleschi completes the dome of the Duomo (1436).
  3. Donatello casts his bronze David (around 1440).
  4. Botticelli paints The Birth of Venus (around 1485).
  5. Michelangelo finishes his marble David (1504).
