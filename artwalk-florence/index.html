Welcome to Florence, the Cradle of the Renaissance

Florence holds one of the richest collections of art in the world. Botticelli, Michelangelo, Leonardo da Vinci and Raphael all worked here, and their masterpieces still fill the city's museums. ArtWalk Florence helps you choose which museums to visit, see what exhibitions are on, compare ticket prices and plan your day.
